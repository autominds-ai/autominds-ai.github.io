The problem

Good leads are slipping away every week

You paid for the advert. You posted the listing. The buyer messaged. Then one of these happened.

Enquiries come in at night

Buyers message after work. By the time someone replies in the morning, many have already called another agent.

Leads go cold

One reply goes out, then nothing. Without follow-up, interested buyers forget about you and your listing.

Hours lost on the same answers

Your team types the price, location and viewing times again and again, instead of spending that time selling.

The faster you reply to an enquiry, the more likely it is to turn into a sale.

What we install

Three systems that keep every enquiry moving

24/7 WhatsApp enquiry assistant

  • Asks about budget, area and timing

Lead capture with alerts

Every lead in one place, and your team knows straight away.

  • Leads from Facebook and Instagram ads, your website and WhatsApp
  • Saved into one sheet or CRM that you own

Follow-up and lead reactivation

Quiet leads get a gentle nudge. Old leads get a second chance.
