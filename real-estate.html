Where agents lose money without noticing

Most agents are not bad at selling. They are just stretched thin. Three things quietly cost you buyers.

  • Slow replies. A buyer who waits often moves on to the next agent.
  • Lost numbers. Enquiries sit in chats, DMs and notes, then get forgotten.
  • No follow-up. One message goes out and nobody checks back.

Each one is a person who already wanted to buy or rent. You paid for the advert. You did the posting. The enquiry arrived. The only thing missing was a quick reply and a gentle nudge.

How the Lead Recovery System works

Here is what happens each time someone enquires about your property.

  1. They message you.From WhatsApp, a form or your adverts.
  2. They get a fast, friendly reply.It asks what they need, such as budget, area and timing.
  3. Their details are saved.Name, number and answers go into one clean sheet.
  4. You get alerted.You see who is serious without digging through chats.
  5. Follow-up goes out on schedule.Quiet leads get a gentle check-in until they reply or say no.
  6. Serious buyers are guided to a viewing.You spend your time on people ready to move.
