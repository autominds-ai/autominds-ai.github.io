How it works

How the Lead Recovery System works

Here is what happens each time someone enquires about your property.

  1. They message you

    From WhatsApp, a form or your adverts.

  2. They get a fast, friendly reply

    It asks what they need, such as budget, area and timing.

  3. Their details are saved

    Name, number and answers go into one clean sheet.

  4. You get alerted

    You see who is serious without digging through chats.

  5. Follow-up goes out on schedule

    Quiet leads get a gentle check-in until they reply or say no.

  6. Serious buyers are guided to a viewing

    You spend your time on people ready to move.
