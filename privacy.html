Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

This policy explains what personal information AutoMinds AI Services collects, why we collect it, who helps us process it, how long we keep it, and how you can ask us to delete it. It covers this website, our free guide form, and the WhatsApp assistants we run, including our demo property assistant.

AutoMinds AI Services is a business registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC) in Lagos, Nigeria. In this policy, "we", "us" and "our" mean AutoMinds AI Services.

1. What we collect

When you use our website or free guide form

  • Your name, phone number and email address
  • Your type of business and the main challenge you choose on the form
  • Which link or campaign brought you to us (for example a flyer or social media link), the page you used, and the time you sent the form

When you message one of our WhatsApp assistants

  • Your WhatsApp phone number and the profile name shown on your WhatsApp account
  • The messages you send and the replies you receive
  • Details you choose to share in the chat, such as the type of property, area, budget, number of bedrooms and when you plan to move
  • A short summary the assistant creates, such as how ready you seem to view a property, and which listings were suggested

Please do not send bank details, ID numbers, health records or passwords in a chat. Our assistants never ask for them.

2. How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and send you the free guide you asked for
  • To suggest suitable options, such as property listings, and help you book a viewing or a call
  • To let a member of our team (or the business you contacted) follow up with you personally
  • To send follow-up emails about our services. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any email
  • To see which of our campaigns work, so we can improve them

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising by other companies.

3. Our WhatsApp assistant uses AI

Our WhatsApp assistants are AI assistants, not people. They tell you so at the start of a conversation. Your messages are sent to an AI model to write a reply. The assistant only shares information it has been given, such as a list of properties, and hands you over to a person for anything it cannot answer. It does not give legal, financial or medical advice.

On our demo property assistant, the property listings are sample listings made up for demonstration. They are not real properties for sale or rent.

5. Systems we build for our clients

When we build an automation for a client business, such as an estate agency or a clinic, the information it collects goes into that client's own accounts. That business decides how the information is used and is responsible for it. To ask about your information in a client's system, please contact that business directly. We will help them respond.

6. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and conversation records only for as long as we need them to reply to you, follow up and run our services. We delete them sooner if you ask us to, as explained below. Records in our demo systems are cleared from time to time.

7. How we protect it

Access to our accounts is limited to our team and protected by passwords. API keys and access tokens are stored in secure settings, not in spreadsheets or public code. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable care to protect your information.

8. Delete your data

You can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, including your WhatsApp conversation with our assistant.

How to ask:

  • Email autominds.ai@zohomail.com with the subject "Delete my data", or
  • Send a WhatsApp message to +234 903 040 5860 saying "Delete my data"

Please include the phone number or email address you used with us, so we can find your records. We will confirm once your information has been deleted from our spreadsheets, email lists and conversation records. To stop our emails straight away, use the unsubscribe link in any email.

Copies of messages kept on your own phone, or by WhatsApp itself, are controlled by you and by WhatsApp, not by us.

9. Children

Our services are for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our services change. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.
