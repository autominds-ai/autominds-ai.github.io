The problem

Busy clinics lose patients without noticing

Your team is focused on the patients in the room. Meanwhile, the phone and the inbox keep going.

Missed calls and DMs

Calls ring out during busy hours and messages wait until the next day. Some of those people book somewhere else.

Empty slots from no-shows

Patients forget their appointment. The slot sits empty, and another patient who wanted it could not get in.

Treatment enquiries go quiet

Someone asks about a treatment, gets one reply, then nothing. Without a follow-up, they never come in.

What we set up

A front desk helper that never closes

You get more booked appointments, fewer empty slots and less time spent on the same questions. We set it all up and look after it for you.

Replies to messages

WhatsApp and Instagram messages get a friendly reply, day and night, so no patient message is left waiting until the next day.

Appointment booking

The assistant offers open slots, collects the patient's name and number, and passes the booking to your team to confirm.

Reminders before appointments

Patients get a reminder before their visit, with an easy way to confirm or move it. This helps reduce no-shows.

Follow-up on treatment enquiries

People who asked about a treatment get a gentle follow-up on a schedule you approve. It stops as soon as they reply or say no.

Review requests

After a visit, patients get a short thank-you message with a link to leave a Google review, if they wish.

Every enquiry in one place

Enquiries are saved into your own spreadsheet or CRM, and your team is alerted when someone needs a person to reply.

Patient details

How patient details are handled

Patient details collected by the system go only to your own accounts, such as your own spreadsheet or CRM. We do not sell or share them. The assistant books appointments and answers common questions. It does not give medical advice.
